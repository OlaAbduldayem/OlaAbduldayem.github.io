03 / 06
Entertainment-Led Campaign Adaptation

Making the campaign idea work
in Arabic, not just translating the line.

Marketing Localization Entertainment Creative Adaptation

The Brief

Adapt entertainment-led product campaigns into Arabic while preserving the creative world behind each launch.

The work included limited-edition product messaging tied to a new entertainment release, as well as social-first concepts connecting product variants with familiar entertainment titles.

The Thinking

The goal was not to mirror the English wording. It was to identify the creative device behind each campaign and rebuild it in Arabic.

Play, competition, flavour, and entertainment cues became the threads connecting product benefits to each campaign world.

Why It Works

The entertainment reference stays recognizable, but the Arabic copy feels written for the audience rather than translated for them.

05 / 06
Scriptwriting & Social Video Concept

Turning a product moment
into a story.

Scriptwriting Concept Development Saudi Dialect

The Brief

Create a light short-form video concept around emotionally charged moments that begin confidently and unravel under pressure.

The Thinking

Build humour through contrast: high expectations, emotional pressure, and a sudden loss of control. The product becomes the moment that resets the scene.

Why It Works

Instead of interrupting the story with a product message, the product is given a role inside the narrative itself.

Scene Excerpt

المشهد: لحظة رومانسية، وهي تنتظر منه الكلام الواثق اللي وعدها به.

هي: يلا حبي، ورّني... إنت الرومانسي الرايق اللي ما يهتز.

هو: نور... قلبي، لبّى عيونك...

هي: هل؟

هو يحاول يكمل... ويتلخبط أكثر.

صوت من خلف الكاميرا: اشرب وكمل... لا تخرب اللحظة.

06 / 06
Self-Initiated Ramadan Campaign · Not Commissioned

Turning a seasonal moment
into a full brand experience.

Campaign Strategy F&B Saudi Audience Content Concepts

The Brief

A self-initiated Ramadan concept proposal developed independently for a Saudi quick-service restaurant brand.

The aim was to go beyond seasonal offers and build a stronger cultural and social connection with the Saudi audience.

The Thinking

Build the campaign around the Ramadan ghabga: not simply as a meal occasion, but as a familiar ritual of gathering, conversation, and connection.

The idea brings that tradition into a modern brand experience.

Why It Works

One cultural insight becomes a flexible campaign system across shared food boxes, social content, competitions, influencer storytelling, packaging, and in-branch experiences.

Big Idea & Selected Copy

غبقة على جوك

بعد صلاة التراويح وقبل السحور تحلى الغبقة.

جمع ربعك والغبقة علينا.

ما تروح العزومة ويدك تطوطح.

دش عليهم ببوكس غبقة ترضي جوهم.

Product Experience

Shared boxes in different sizes were designed around small and large gatherings, supported by Ramadan-inspired packaging and gifting ideas.

Content Ecosystem

The campaign expands into heritage stories, social challenges, Ramadan traditions, user participation, and content built around gathering moments.

Activation

The proposal also explored influencer storytelling, selected in-branch experiences, competitions, and interactive ideas designed for different audience segments.
